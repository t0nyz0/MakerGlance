# MakerGlance 3.9.9

- **See all recorded changes on model cards.** Dashboard cards can show up to five pills for likes, downloads, boosts, prints and saves in the selected period. The Models grid shows all five types of positive growth. The cover area can grow to fit the pills at larger text sizes.
- **Clear the unread count by opening MakerGlance.** Opening the main window or menu bar panel marks existing notifications as read. The updates stay in Activity, and new updates can still appear as unread while the window or panel is open.
- **Scroll through all your activity from the menu bar.** The menu bar panel shows the full saved activity list. If ten updates came in, all ten are available to scroll through.

Build 37 is available for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. The ZIP and DMG use ad hoc signing and do not have Apple notarization. macOS may ask again for access to the saved Bambu and MakerWorld direct message credentials after installation.
