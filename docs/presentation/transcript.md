# Slide 9

MAKERGLANCE / TRUST & BOUNDARIES

Local data. Clear boundaries.

History on your Mac

SQLite observations and preferences. The app does
not encrypt the database.

Sessions in Keychain

User-initiated sign-in, creator validation and no saved
password.

Read-only monitoring

No redemption, withdrawal, transfer or
server-message-read actions.

Undocumented services

Availability and schemas can change. Missing data and
reasons stay explicit.

Independent, unofficial companion. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bambu Lab or MakerWorld.
